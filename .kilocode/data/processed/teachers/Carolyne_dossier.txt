- Commitment Target: In next Tuesday's Science lesson with Year 4 after school holidays. I will focus on upgrading the peer talk element. Instead of just letting them talk to a partner freely, I will introduce simple sentence frames for example agree with you because... or My idea is different because... to help them structure their partner dialogue more effectively
- Internal Shift Observed: In my Year 4 Science class, during our discussion on animal classification, I consciously stopped the habit of stepping in to answer my own questions when met with silence. Instead, I stood at the front, counted to 5 slowly in my head, and gave them space to think. At first, the silence felt a bit uncomfortable for them, but because wait time is becoming more habitual for me, the students realized I wasn't going to bail them out. Two students who rarely volunteer ended up offering really thoughtful insights because they finally had the time to process the question.
