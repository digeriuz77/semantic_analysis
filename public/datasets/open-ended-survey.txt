Open-Ended Survey Responses: "What was your biggest frustration with the new feature?"

The notifications are way too frequent. I get pinged for every little thing and it made me turn them all off, which means I miss the ones that actually matter.

I wanted to like it but it crashed twice during onboarding. Lost my progress both times. Not great for a first impression.

Honestly the feature itself is fine, the problem is no one explained how to use it. I had to hunt through help docs to figure out the basic workflow. A short tutorial would have saved me an hour.

Speed is my main complaint. Everything feels sluggish, especially on mobile data. I'm tapping things and waiting, which makes it feel broken even when it isn't.

I love the idea but the pricing surprised me. It wasn't clear during sign-up that the good parts were locked behind a paywall. Felt a bit bait and switch.

Search is useless. I type exactly what I'm looking for and it returns unrelated results. Half the time I can't find my own saved items.

The interface is clean but too minimal — there are no labels on the icons and I keep guessing what they do. Accessibility seems like an afterthought.

It's reliable now, which I appreciate. The earlier version was buggy and I'd given up on it, but this release actually works. Keep that stability going.

Customer support never replied to my ticket. Twelve days and counting. The product might be improving but if I can't get help when something breaks I can't trust it for work.

More customization please. The defaults assume one way of working and I don't fit that mold. Let me rearrange the dashboard and hide what I don't use.
